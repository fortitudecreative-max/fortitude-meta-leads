Measurement Definitions

Signed at kickoff. If we don't agree up front on what a lead, a booking and a close are, nobody can prove anything in month three, and that's how the last agency lost them.

Measurement Definitions Agreement

Fortitude Creative and the client agree to measure the engagement with the definitions below, from the same data sources, before and after launch.

TermDefinitionSource of truth
LeadAny inbound phone call over seconds, form submission, chat or LSA message from a unique contact within a 7-day window.
JunkA lead that is spam, a job seeker, a vendor or solicitor, outside the service area, or for a service the client doesn't offer.
Real leadLead − junk.—
Booking rateAppointments set ÷ real leads.
Close rateJobs sold ÷ appointments run (cancellations and no-shows excluded from the denominator and tracked separately).
Cost per booked jobMarketing spend in the period ÷ appointments set from paid sources.—
Cost per acquired customerMarketing spend in the period ÷ new customers with a sold job.—
Missed-call rateInbound calls not answered by a person within rings, and not returned within 15 minutes ÷ all inbound calls.

Baseline

Measured over the days before kickoff, using the sources above.

What else changes

List anything else that will change during the engagement (new CSR, price change, new service line, ad budget change), so results are attributed honestly.

These definitions describe how results are measured. They are not a guarantee of any booking rate, lead count, revenue or ranking.
