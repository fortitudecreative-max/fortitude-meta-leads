Field Card

Keep it next to the phone · Fortitude Creative

The chain

Market→Positioning→Brand→Trust→Conversion→Booking→Close→Revenue

The point isn't looking nicer. It's that more of the traffic you already pay for turns into booked, closed, profitable work.

Call 1 · Discovery (20–30 min)

No teaching until you have 3 numbers: spend/CPL · booking rate · close rate (+ ticket mix). Talk <40%.

  1. Why reach out now?
  2. Where do jobs come from: referral / paid / organic?
  3. Monthly spend? CPL or cost per booked job?
  4. Service ticket, install ticket, % installs?
  5. Booking rate (appts ÷ real leads)?
  6. Close rate?
  7. Gross margin?
  8. % service → replacement in 2 yrs? Memberships?
  9. Phone rings mid-job, nobody answers: then what?
  10. How many techs? How hard is hiring?
  11. What did you try that didn't work? (agency = delegator, DIY = controller)
  12. Who else decides?
  13. This quarter or next year?

Close: read it back in their words. Book Call 2 with the partner on the invite.

Call 2 · Diagnosis (45 min)

  • 3 Mirror: "Still accurate?"
  • 5 Trust score: let the low scores speak
  • 15 Three leaks: only three
  • 10 Economics on their numbers, label assumptions
  • 10 Plan + persona relief
  • 3 One matching case study
  • 3 Price. Then stop talking.

Does this feel like the right direction for where you want to take the business?

Then offer two dated kickoff options.

Calculator lock

More than 2 core inputs are benchmarks → no dollar figures. Untagged counts as benchmark.

Route, don't menu

  • Spend up, results flat / lose on price / can't hire / exit → Full brand
  • Calls don't book → Full brand + CSR note, or capture first
  • AI logo / unsure / controller → Paid Audit
  • Needs leads this month → Tier 0
  • Phones unanswered / no-show reviews → Capture first, brand in shoulder season
  • Too small, no plan → Honest no

At your size, I'd spend that money on a CSR and a service van before hiring us.

Persona close

Delegator ("we", hired an agency):

We own almost all of it. Your part is a kickoff, two review rounds, approvals within five business days.

Controller ("I", did it themselves):

You approve the positioning before a single sketch. Nothing goes on a truck you didn't sign.

Unclear? Use neither. Book the next meeting.

Objections, in one line

  • Lot of money: "Compared to what? Doing nothing has a number too. It's on the screen."
  • Have a logo: "Is it doing any work? If it's strong, we'll sell you the rollout instead."
  • AI's fine: "For a mood board, sure. Ask it what a [ZIP] homeowner fears in July."
  • Word of mouth: "Referrals Google you before they call. What do they see?"
  • Agency failed: "No tracking or no strategy? What happened?" Then listen.
  • Think about it: "Price, timing, whether it'll work, or something else?"
  • Partner: "What'll their biggest question be?" Then offer a 15-min call with both.
  • Cheaper?: "The audit or the capture fix. Brand in the shoulder season."
  • Next year: "Start in [month − 2], live by the season."
  • Cheaper agency: "Research before design? Strategy tied to my market? Ownership in writing?"

Never

  • Guarantee booking rate, revenue, leads or rankings
  • "#1 on Google" · "save $50 a click"
  • Imply a brand alone makes the phone ring
  • Competitor lawsuits · unverified competitor pricing · "same exact work"
  • Trademark opinions beyond "ask your attorney"
  • Dates production hasn't confirmed
  • "$100K/month" unless derived from their numbers
  • Give away, before payment: the positioning answer, the brand story, the messaging, the white space, any visual direction

Every call ends with

I'll send the agreement today. Kickoff the 3rd or the 10th?

…or at minimum, a dated next step.
